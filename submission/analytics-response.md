# Analytics response

Measure whether visitors reach the appropriate initiative and take a meaningful next step. Track hero destinations, ecosystem selections, verified project and brand links, speaking enquiries and contact routes. Count form submissions only if a real submission flow is introduced. Exclude personal information and honour Do Not Track.

Use GA4 for acquisition and journey analysis, Search Console for search intent and discoverability, Lighthouse for repeatable performance diagnostics, and Clarity only after appropriate privacy review and consent configuration. Unavailable placeholder controls must never count as enquiries.

With 5,000 visitors and five enquiries, the observed enquiry rate is 0.1%. First confirm that both measurements are accurate and refer to the same period. Segment visitors by source, campaign, device, geography, landing intent and returning status. A research audience may be valuable without seeking an architecture commission, so assess each pathway separately.

Inspect the funnel from entry to work, initiative selection, enquiry route and verified destination. Identify exits, confusing controls and mobile friction. Check broken links, unavailable addresses, JavaScript errors, loading performance, accessibility, spam filtering and whether enquiry tracking is duplicated or missing.

Then test a clearer Studio COKA project CTA, stronger verified project evidence and a shorter speaking booking journey. Compare qualified enquiries by segment, not only total clicks. Define success and a minimum observation period before each experiment; change one major variable at a time. Use findings to improve content hierarchy and remove friction rather than chasing traffic volume alone.
